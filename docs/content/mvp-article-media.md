# MVP article instructional media

The nine new JPEG assets below were created on 2026-08-27 with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool, then exported at JPEG quality 84 to match the project's existing article-media convention. They are original generated teaching visuals, not hotlinked or copied source images.

All prompts used the `scientific-educational` use case and requested photorealistic editorial instruction photography with no text, labels, arrows, branding, logos, or watermarks.

| Asset | Article / teaching purpose | Prompt summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `touch-up-paint-comparison.jpg` | Distinguish color matching from sheen and roller-texture mismatch. | Two-part residential wall comparison: an angled-light touch-up view and a close texture view, with plausible fine stipple and no branded products. |
| `paint-flashing-comparison.jpg` | Show flashing under raking light. | A pale painted wall with several subtle, smooth repair-shaped patches reflecting differently from the surrounding rolled finish. |
| `caulk-or-filler-comparison.jpg` | Contrast a rigid trim defect with a flexible joint. | Equal close views of a nail hole in the face of white baseboard and a narrow baseboard-to-wall joint. |
| `trim-gap-comparison.jpg` | Contrast a cosmetic joint with a loose-trim repair problem. | Equal close views of a narrow uniform wall gap and a larger irregular separation where baseboard pulls away. |
| `drywall-repair-scale-comparison.jpg` | Show direct fill, patch, and cut-and-replace scales. | Three drywall panels progressing from a nail hole to a mesh patch and a squared supported replacement piece, without exact size labels or utilities. |
| `acrylic-water-consistency-comparison.jpg` | Show how water changes acrylic body and transparency. | Three same-color teal swatches progressing from full-bodied opaque paint through slightly loosened paint to a transparent watery application. |
| `acrylic-brush-moisture-comparison.jpg` | Compare dry, damp, and dripping brushes. | Three identical flat brushes above broken, controlled opaque, and watery pooling teal strokes. |
| `acrylic-blending-sequence.jpg` | Teach a three-stage gradient workflow. | Three canvas panels showing separate teal and yellow bands, a workable green overlap, and a softly blended painterly transition. |
| `acrylic-color-mixing-comparison.jpg` | Compare deliberate two-color mixing with accidental overmixing. | A clean teal-and-yellow mixture producing vivid green beside several unrelated remnants mixed to a useful but less saturated gray-brown neutral. |

The veneer article reuses `public/images/article-guides/furniture-surface-comparison.jpg`, an existing accurate comparison of solid wood, thin real-wood veneer over a substrate, and laminate over particleboard. Reuse avoids a redundant generated asset and follows the package's `reuse-ok` instruction.
