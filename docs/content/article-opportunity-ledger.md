## Field meanings

- **Evidence:** `package` is the approved MVP editorial package; `repo` is the audited live content baseline; `deferred` is a researched next-rotation opportunity.
- **Ranking:** lower numbers indicate higher current editorial priority within a batch. Baseline entries use `—` until the next evidence-led ranking review.
- **Featured:** current promotion intent, not publication status.
- **Movement:** rising, stable, or falling. New articles begin with the package's editorial signal; baseline and deferred topics begin stable.
- **Collision / owner:** the route that owns the intent or the nearest route that must be checked before work begins.

## Monthly review procedure

1. Recheck intent collisions and established owners before proposing a route.
2. Review Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, and queries once meaningful data exists.
3. Re-rank opportunities using PFB evidence first, external demand second, and editorial usefulness as the final constraint.
4. Record movement and the review date even when no action changes.
5. Change `featured` only as a promotion decision; never use `isArchived` to mean “not on a landing page.”
