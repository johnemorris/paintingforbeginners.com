Some brush texture is normal. Deep ridges and obvious tracks are different.

If the paint is already dry, the solution is usually to **level the defect before recoating**, not bury it under more paint.

## First decide whether it is actually a problem

Look at the surface under normal room lighting from a normal viewing distance.

Trim painted with a brush will not necessarily look identical to a sprayed factory finish.

If the marks are only visible when you put a flashlight against the surface, you may be fixing something nobody will notice.

## If the ridges are obvious

Let the coating dry and harden enough for the repair method recommended for that product.

Then:

1. lightly smooth the raised brush ridges
2. feather the surrounding area
3. remove sanding dust
4. apply another uniform coat

Do not sand so aggressively that you expose the substrate unless that is intentionally part of the repair.

## Why brush marks happen

### The brush was too dry

A starved brush drags instead of releasing a smooth film.

### Too much pressure

Pressing hard spreads the bristles and can carve tracks into the wet coating.

### Too many finishing strokes

Paint needs a chance to level. Constantly brushing an area as it begins to set can leave permanent texture.

### The wrong brush or coating combination

Use a brush type recommended for the coating and task.

### Heavy paint buildup

A thick ridge has more material to level and can sag or hold brush texture.

## Separate normal tool texture from a defect

Brush, roller, and spray application do not produce identical textures. Judge the result expected for the chosen coating and tool before trying to make one method imitate another.

## Do not add thinner automatically

If a coating feels difficult to brush, do not start adding water or solvent based on internet folklore.

Only thin or modify paint when the manufacturer allows it, and stay within the specified limits.

## The prevention rule

Load the brush properly, spread the paint, make the minimum controlled finishing strokes you need, and then **stop touching it**.
